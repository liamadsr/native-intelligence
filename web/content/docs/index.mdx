Welcome to the Native Intelligence documentation. Native Intelligence is one workspace for your whole company: meetings, mail, chat, docs, tasks and customers live in one app and share one memory, and agents use that memory to take work off your plate.

## What is Native Intelligence?

Most teams switch between a mail client, a chat app, a notes tool, a task tracker, a CRM and a meeting recorder, and what they learn in one is lost to the others. Native Intelligence puts that work in one place. Everything your team says in calls, writes in mail and chat, and records in docs feeds a shared memory of what was decided, promised and learned, each with its source. Agents read that memory to answer questions, turn commitments into tasks, keep projects and customer records current, and take on work themselves.

## What's in the workspace

- **[Mail](/docs/features/email)** — Gmail and Outlook in one inbox, with drafted replies, send later, and tasks from a thread
- **Team chat and calls** — Channels, direct messages and calls for your team
- **[Calendar](/docs/features/calendar)** — Google and Microsoft calendars in one view, plus booking links
- **[Captures](/docs/features/captures)** — Meetings and calls captured without a bot, with transcripts, notes, and the tasks and decisions they produced
- **[Actions](/docs/features/actions)** — Tasks with custom workflows, priorities, assignees and team-scoped identifiers (like ENG-42)
- **[Projects and initiatives](/docs/features/projects-and-initiatives)** — Work organized into projects with milestones, under strategic initiatives
- **[Documents](/docs/features/documents)** — Rich-text docs, alongside tables and files in one drive
- **[Contacts and CRM](/docs/features/contacts-and-crm)** — Companies, contacts, deals and interaction history, filled in from your calls
- **[AI assistant](/docs/features/ai-assistant)** — Ask anything about your workspace, and have it draft, file and update for you
- **Agents and flows** — Agents with skills you teach them, and flows that run on triggers or on a schedule
- **[Studio](/docs/features/studio)** and **[SEO](/docs/features/seo)** — Videos and ads, and your website's search performance
- **[Views](/docs/features/views-and-filters)** and **[Search](/docs/features/search)** — Filtered views in list and board layouts, and search across the workspace
- **[Desktop app](/docs/desktop-app)** — The macOS app for bot-free meeting capture and the live agent (Windows coming)
