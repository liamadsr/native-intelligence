Workspaces and teams are the organizational foundation of Native Intelligence. Everything you create — actions, projects, captures, contacts — belongs to a workspace, and teams provide scoping within it.

## Workspaces

A workspace is a shared environment for your organization. Each workspace has:

- **Members** with role-based access (Owner, Admin, Member, Guest)
- **Teams** that organize work into logical groups
- **Settings** for labels, workflow states, integrations, and preferences
- **A plan** (Free, Pro, or Enterprise) that determines available features

### Workspace Settings

Access workspace settings from the sidebar gear icon:

- **General** — Name, logo, and workspace-level preferences
- **Members** — Invite, remove, and manage member roles
- **Teams** — Create and configure teams
- **Labels** — Define workspace-wide labels for actions, projects, and initiatives
- **Connectors** — Connect email, calendar and other apps, and MCP servers
- **Library** — Browse and manage your Skills, Agents, and Meeting Types (lives at the dashboard level, not in settings)

### Switching Workspaces

If you belong to multiple workspaces, use the workspace switcher in the sidebar to move between them. Each workspace has its own data, members, and settings.

Two workspaces that should be one can be merged if you own both: everything in one moves into the other, and nothing of yours is lost. See [Merge Workspaces](/docs/configuration/merge-workspaces).

## Teams

Teams are groups within a workspace that scope work. Each team has:

- **An issue prefix** — A short code (like `ENG`, `SALES`, `DESIGN`) used in action identifiers
- **Members** — Workspace members assigned to the team, with a lead or member role
- **Workflow states** — Custom statuses that define how work moves through your process
- **An action counter** — Automatically increments to generate identifiers like `ENG-42`

### Creating a Team

1. Go to **Settings → Teams**
2. Click **Create Team**
3. Set a name and issue prefix
4. Add team members and assign a team lead
5. Customize workflow states if needed

### Workflow States

Each team has its own set of workflow states organized into categories:

| Category | Purpose | Examples |
|----------|---------|----------|
| **Backlog** | Work not yet planned | Backlog, Triage |
| **Unstarted** | Planned but not begun | Todo, Ready |
| **Started** | Actively being worked on | In Progress, In Review |
| **Completed** | Successfully finished | Done, Shipped |
| **Canceled** | Decided not to do | Canceled, Won't Do |

Teams come with sensible defaults, but you can add, rename, reorder, or remove states to match your workflow.

### Team Roles

| Role | Capabilities |
|------|-------------|
| **Lead** | Full team management, assign work, manage workflow states |
| **Member** | Create and work on actions within the team |

## Invitations

Invite people to your workspace from **Settings → Workspace → Members**. Invitations are sent via email with a unique token link. Pending invitations can be resent or revoked.

When someone accepts an invite, they become a workspace member and can be added to any team.
