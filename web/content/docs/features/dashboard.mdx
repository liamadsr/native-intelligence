The Home dashboard and My Actions views are your starting points in Dial8 — they surface the work that matters to you right now.

## Home Dashboard

The Home view gives you a snapshot of your workspace:

### My Actions

See your assigned actions organized by status:
- **In Progress** — Work you're actively doing
- **Upcoming** — Actions due soon
- **Overdue** — Past-due items that need attention
- **Recently Completed** — What you've shipped

### Up Next

Your next calendar event for today, with a direct link to join or view details.

### Upcoming Meetings

Upcoming meetings from your connected calendars, showing auto-capture status for each. Meetings with 2+ attendees can automatically create pending captures for recording.

### Pending Review

Captures that have been processed by AI but need your review — confirming extracted actions and decisions before they become real workspace items.

### Active Cycles

Current sprint/cycle progress for your teams, showing completion metrics and how many actions are done vs. remaining.

### My Projects

Projects you're involved in, with milestone progress bars and counts of open and overdue actions.

### Recent Captures

Your latest meeting recordings and their processing status.

## My Actions (Todo)

The My Actions view is a focused task manager for your personal workload, accessible from the sidebar.

### Scope Tabs

- **Assigned** — Actions assigned to you
- **Created** — Actions you created (regardless of assignee)

### Filter Tabs

- **All** — Everything
- **Today** — Due today
- **This Week** — Due this week
- **Overdue** — Past due date
- **Completed** — Finished items

### Quick Actions

- Add new actions inline from the todo view
- Toggle completion status with a single click
- See priority, due date, project, labels, assignee, and comment count at a glance

## Onboarding

When you're new, your assistant walks you through Dial8 in the chat. It checks what it learned about you and your company while you signed up, helps you record a first capture and shows you what came out of it, then offers to connect your calendar. Say "skip" any time to stop the walk-through, or type `/getting-started` later to run it again.
