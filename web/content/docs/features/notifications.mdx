---
title: Notifications
description: How Dial8 tells you what happened — your agent says it in the chat, with a banner on the desktop.
---

Dial8 has no separate notifications page. When something happens that you should know about, your agent hears about it and tells you in your chat.

## What your agent tells you right away

Within a couple of minutes, your agent writes in your chat about:

- **Someone wants you** — a reply to your comment, a comment on something of yours, an action assigned to you, or a capture or document shared with you
- **Reminders you set** — "Remind me about this" came due
- **Something failed** — a background job or an outreach sequence of yours

Several at once arrive as one message.

## What waits for later

Some things don't need you right now. Your agent mentions them on your next message, and your morning summary (8:00 your time) lists them:

- **Teammates' changes** — an action you're on was completed or reopened, you were unassigned, a project you lead changed status, a sprint started, a decision from your capture was confirmed
- **Follow-ups** — a contact's follow-up date is today or tomorrow

## Banners

In the desktop app, each notification also shows as a system banner while Dial8 isn't in front (or a small toast while it is). Clicking a banner opens what it's about. If there's nothing particular to open, it opens your chat. A banner you've clicked counts as seen, so your agent won't bring it up again.

An agent's own updates (its drafts, reviews and results) also live on its page.

## Asking what you missed

Ask your agent "what did I miss?" and it reads back what happened in the last week, including what it already told you.

## Settings

Sound effects are in **Settings → Preferences**. There are no per-type notification settings: your agent decides what is worth telling you now and what can wait.

## Retention

Dial8 keeps a notification for 30 days after you've seen it, and for 90 days at most.
