title: Contacts & CRM
description: Full relationship management with interaction history, company tracking, visibility controls, and voice identification.
---

Dial8 includes a built-in CRM for tracking the people and companies you work with. Contacts are connected to email threads, meeting captures, and actions — giving you a complete picture of every relationship.

## Contacts

### Contact Properties

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Name** | First and last name |
| **Email** | Email address (used for auto-linking) |
| **Phone** | Phone number |
| **Company** | Associated company |
| **Job title** | Role at their company |
| **Relationship type** | Client, Partner, Vendor, Colleague, etc. |
| **Relationship strength** | Strong, Warm, Neutral, Cold, or Stale |
| **Notes** | Free-text notes about the contact |
| **Tags** | Labels for categorization |

### Creating Contacts

- **Manually** — Create from the contacts view
- **From captures** — Participants in captures can be linked or created as contacts
- **From calendar** — Sync attendees from calendar events as contacts
- **From email** — Contacts are auto-linked based on email addresses

### Visibility & Sharing

Contacts have configurable visibility:

| Visibility | Who can see |
|------------|------------|
| **Private** | Only the creator |
| **Team** | Members of specified teams |
| **Workspace** | Everyone in the workspace |

Share specific contacts with individual members or teams, with view or edit access levels.

### Interaction History

Every contact has a timeline of interactions:

- **Captures** — Meetings they participated in
- **Emails** — Email threads involving their address
- **Actions** — Tasks linked to or about them
- **Notes** — Manual interaction entries

This timeline is built automatically as you use Dial8 — no manual logging required.

### Comments

Add comments to contacts for internal notes and discussion. Comments support threading and @mentions.

## Companies

Group contacts by the companies they belong to.

### Company Properties

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Name** | Company name |
| **Domain** | Website domain (used for auto-association) |
| **Industry** | Business sector |
| **Employee count** | Company size |
| **Logo** | Company logo URL |
| **Description** | About the company |

### Company-Contact Relationship

Assign contacts to companies to see all your relationships at an organization in one place. Company views aggregate interaction data across all associated contacts.

## Voice Identification

With the [desktop app](/docs/desktop-app), Dial8 can identify speakers in meetings by their voice:

- **Voice enrollment** — Store voice embeddings (voiceprints) for contacts
- **Automatic matching** — During captures, speakers are matched to enrolled contacts
- **Progressive trust** — The system tracks confidence scores and improves with corrections
- **Manual correction** — Reassign speaker segments when automatic matching is wrong

This means your meeting transcripts can automatically attribute who said what, linked directly to CRM contacts.

## Filtering & Views

Filter contacts by:
- Company, relationship type, relationship strength
- Tags, last interaction date, follow-up date
- Created by, visibility level

Save filters as [custom views](/docs/features/views-and-filters) for quick access.
