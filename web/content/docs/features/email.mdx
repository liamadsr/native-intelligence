Native Intelligence brings your email into your workspace with a unified inbox that connects threads to contacts, actions, and projects.

## Connected Accounts

Connect one or more email accounts from **Settings → Connectors**:

- **Gmail** — Full inbox sync via Google OAuth
- **Outlook** — Full inbox sync via Microsoft OAuth

Each connected account syncs independently. You can configure visibility and notification preferences per account in **Settings → Email & CRM**.

## Email Threads

Emails are organized as threads, matching the conversation threading from your email provider. Each thread shows:

- **Subject and participants**
- **All messages** in the conversation with full bodies
- **Attachments** with metadata and download links
- **Labels** — Apply workspace labels to threads for organization
- **Status indicators** — Unread, starred, needs reply

## Inbox Management

The email view supports standard inbox operations:

- **Read / Unread** — Mark threads as read or unread
- **Star** — Flag important threads
- **Archive** — Remove from inbox without deleting
- **Trash** — Move to trash
- **Labels** — Categorize with workspace labels
- **Folders** — Browse by folder (Inbox, Sent, Drafts, etc.)

## Search

Search across all your email messages with full-text search. Results show highlighted matches within message bodies.

## AI-Drafted Emails

Native Intelligence can generate email drafts using AI, with context pulled from your workspace:

- **Reply drafts** — Click **Reply All** on a thread (or the reply button on one message) and the assistant drafts a reply in the chat, based on the thread and related workspace data
- **New drafts** — Compose emails with AI assistance
- **Context sources** — AI references relevant captures, actions, contacts, and projects when drafting

Drafts are saved for review before sending. Edit, approve, or discard them. A draft waiting in a thread (an agent's reply, for example) shows a **Draft** tag with **Send**, which sends it exactly as you reviewed it; **Edit**, which lets you change the recipients, subject and text before sending; and **Discard**, which deletes it from your mailbox once you confirm. An edited draft goes out as plain text, and a draft with attachments can only be sent as it is.

## Contact Linking

Email messages are automatically linked to workspace contacts based on email addresses. This means:

- Viewing a contact shows their email history
- Viewing an email thread shows linked contact profiles
- Email interactions contribute to the contact's interaction timeline

## Action Linking

Link email messages to specific actions to maintain a clear trail from conversation to task.

## Sync

Email syncing happens automatically in the background. Each account tracks its sync progress independently using provider-specific mechanisms (Gmail history IDs, Outlook delta links). You can also trigger a manual sync from the settings.

### Webhooks

Native Intelligence supports push notifications from email providers for real-time updates:

- **Gmail** — Webhook push notifications for new messages
- **Outlook** — Subscription-based change notifications
