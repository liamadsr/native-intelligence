Every new workspace starts with a 14-day free trial — all features unlocked, no credit card required. Paid plans pick up from there.

## Free Trial

All features are unlocked for **14 days** when you create your workspace. No credit card required.

## Free Plan

Core workspace features at no cost:

- Workspaces and teams
- Actions with custom workflows
- Projects, initiatives, and milestones
- Documents
- Contacts and CRM
- Calendar view
- Search
- Labels and workflow customization
- AI assistant (basic — Claude Haiku)

## Pro Plan

Unlock the full Dial8 experience:

- All free features
- Expanded AI models (Claude Sonnet and more)
- Unlimited captures with AI processing
- Multi-account email and calendar integration
- AI email drafting with workspace context
- Semantic search across captures
- Shared views across teams
- Voice ID for speaker identification
- Priority processing

Pro is a monthly subscription with managed AI access — no API keys or setup required. Payments are processed securely via Stripe.
