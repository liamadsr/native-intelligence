**Your second brain. Always on.**

We've all been there. A meeting ends with ten action items and no one writes them down. A conversation sparks an important insight but by the time you switch to another tool, you've lost the thread. Your inbox, calendar, and notes live in three different places and none of them talk to each other.

We built Native Intelligence because the gap between thinking and doing is where good ideas go to die.

## The Problem with Productivity Tools

Most productivity software asks you to change how you work. It demands you adopt a new system, fill in the right fields, tag things correctly, and remember to open the app in the first place.

We think that's backwards.

Your brain doesn't work in structured forms. It works in conversation. In the middle of a call. Across a dozen browser tabs. The tool should adapt to you — not the other way around.

## What Native Intelligence Is

Native Intelligence is a voice-first AI platform that lives on your desktop, designed to capture, process, and act on your thoughts — automatically.

Speak naturally. Native Intelligence listens. It transcribes your words, surfaces what matters, extracts action items, and connects everything into a living system that actually keeps up with how you think.

Think of it as the intelligent layer between your brain and everything you need to get done.

## What Makes It Different

**Voice-first, not voice-added.** Most tools bolt on voice as an afterthought. We designed Native Intelligence from the ground up for speech — because that's how humans actually communicate, especially in the flow of work.

**It captures meetings, not just notes.** Native Intelligence sits alongside your conversations, automatically surfacing decisions, commitments, and follow-ups — so you can stay present instead of furiously typing.

**An AI that thinks ahead.** Native Intelligence doesn't just record — it suggests. When it spots a task, a follow-up, or a decision that needs tracking, it surfaces it for you. No more manually combing through notes to figure out what needs to happen next.

**Everything in one place.** Email, calendar, contacts, notes, tasks — all feeding into a single intelligent system that understands your context and helps you move faster.

## Built for the Way You Actually Work

Native Intelligence isn't for a specific type of person. It's for anyone running a team, managing a complex project, or sitting through back-to-back meetings who wants to spend less time organizing and more time doing.

If your work happens at a desk — on calls, in documents, across tools — Native Intelligence is designed to be the one thing that makes all of it click.

We've been building this for ourselves, and now we're ready to share it.
